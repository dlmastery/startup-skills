Per beachhead segment (personas cards 2–3; card 1 is harvested by the same funnel via content, not sold to; cards 4–5 appear only in Grow — the year-2+ expand motion, market_type §4). Honest baseline everywhere: **current = 0 / not yet measurable** (A4, zero external users). Targets are the pack's own committed numbers, not new inventions.

## Segment A — Marcus: solo quant researchers / small prop shops (card 2)

### Get
- **Channel → activation path:** the **Reproducible Champion loop** (gtm §2): published DSR-honest bundle → skeptic reproduces with one command → reproduction converts to signup at zero marginal CAC. Feeder channels: autoresearch issue/fork community (gtm §3 row 1), r/quant + QuantConnect/Numerai (plan-B channel, gtm §4).
- **Instrumentation — exactly three numbers (gtm §2, kept identical):** bundles published/mo × reproductions/bundle × reproduction→signup rate. These three are the growth model; nothing else counts as acquisition data. **How they're actually measured (D-TELEMETRY):** a minimal **opt-in reproduction-registry ping** — one line of consent shown at `ascent reproduce` time — plus GitHub clone stats. This undercounts by construction (opt-in rates assumed 20–50%); all reported numbers carry the frozen correction band from experiment_board E3. "Instrumented from day 1" means the registry ships in v0, not that we see everything.
- **Activation moment (declared):** **the user's first own-data campaign that survives the gates** — not signup, not clone, not a public-bundle rerun. A public-bundle reproduction is a *conversion* event; activation is when Ascent produces an audited kept improvement on *their* private data (the contamination-proof act that public bundles can't be, gtm §2.2). **Measurement honesty:** own-data campaigns are private by definition, so activation is counted via the registry ping plus a short user survey until registry density suffices — early activation counts are registry-plus-self-report estimates, not a census, and are labeled as such wherever cited.
- Metric: activated users. Current: 0. Target: trust test seeds ≥3 (E1b, sessions weeks 10–13); pre-order window days 110–120: 30 pre-orders (E4); month-12: 150 paying (market_sizing §5). Lever: bundle quality + the one-command reproduction path (fix the artifact, not the marketing — gtm §4 gate).

### Keep
- **The habit loop:** overnight campaign runs → **morning dashboard check** (the founder-observed usage rhythm, paper App. B.3) → steer ("pivot", "self-reflect on stall") → next overnight run. The product's session structure (hours–days autonomous runs, BRIEF) makes the check-steer-rerun cycle the natural daily habit; the dashboard is the habit surface BYOK Pro sells.
- **Retention-predicting metric (declared now, validated against churn later): campaigns/week per user + reproduction shares** (bundles the user shares that others rerun). Basis: a user with a standing campaign has recurring value flowing; a user who *shares* reproducible bundles has staked reputation on the tool (assumption: declared 2026-08-27, to be validated on the first two paying cohorts). Threshold to watch: users at ≥1 campaign/week in weeks 2–4 post-activation are provisionally "retained"; below that, churn-risk outreach.
- Metric: week-4 retention of activated users. Current: n/a. Target: set after first cohort (no invented benchmark). Lever: steering copilot quality + campaign-stall diagnostics (the "self-reflect on stall" moment is where habit dies).

### Grow
- **Referral mechanism = the bundle itself:** every shared winner archive is a proof artifact + acquisition asset (gtm §2 — content that ranks for "<dataset> SOTA"). Target, costed against capacity (critic round 2): **8–10 founder-published bundles by day 180, ≥3 reproductions each; community-published bundles counted as upside, not plan.** The arithmetic: gtm §4 budgets 10 hrs/wk for bundles + DSR/CPCV computation + posts; a DSR-honest bundle (ledger export, deflation computation, write-up with the contamination caveat) costs ~15–20 of those hours, so ~26 weeks of that workstream yields 8–10, not the 30 the gtm draft assumed — the 30-bundle figure quietly presumed community contribution that hasn't been earned yet.
- **Lifetime-Pro trust-test alumni** (gtm §4 incentive): 15 named, credibility-selected practitioners whose acknowledgment is the referral seed — they are the reproduction network's first nodes (A11 asset 2).
- Expansion: solo → 2-person shop = second seat; the shared-archive need is the Team trigger.

- Lever: documentation-writes-itself quality ("The documentation writes itself or it doesn't get written" — must-have language, card 3).

**Decision forced:** the funnel's single point of failure is the reproduction step — it is simultaneously Get-conversion, the E1 pass condition, and the referral mechanic. Next action: make one-command reproduction bulletproof on a fresh machine (Windows/Mac/Linux) *before* any bundle publicity; a bundle that fails to reproduce for a skeptic is negative CAC in a trust business. Cost stated, not assumed away: bulletproof fresh-machine reproduce is **weeks of packaging work** (env pinning, cross-OS deps, offline DSR script) — it is costed inside the trust-test kernel build (weeks 1–9, D-CALENDAR), which is exactly why the E1 sessions slid to weeks 10–13 rather than pretending the packaging is free.
