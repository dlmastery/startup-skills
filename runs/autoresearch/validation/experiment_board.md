## E1 operating characteristics (computed, not hand-waved)

The thresholds are frozen; know what they can and cannot detect at n=15. Leg (a), ≥5/15 reproducing: if the true reproduction rate were only 0.20, the test still passes ~16% of the time (Binomial(15, 0.20), P(X≥5) ≈ 0.164) — a ~16% false-pass risk against a mediocre artifact; if the true rate is 0.50, power to pass is ~94% (P(X≥5 | p=0.5) ≈ 0.941). The AND-of-three structure compounds false-*fail* risk: at healthy true rates of 0.50 / 0.30 / 0.60 for legs (a)/(b)/(c), the per-leg pass probabilities are ≈ 0.94 / 0.87 / 0.79, whose product is ≈ 0.65 — i.e. up to a ~35% chance of failing the joint test even when all three underlying rates are healthy, under independence; the three legs share the same 15 people and are positively correlated, which pulls the joint false-fail below that bound but not to zero. Consequence, pre-committed: a *narrow* miss (one leg short by one count) triggers a diagnostic review against these curves before the fail branch executes — but the thresholds themselves do not move (board rule 2).

## Board rules

1. **No row moves from "planned" without raw counts attached** — "went well" is not a result (quality-bar #1: mechanism over adjective; SKILL red flag: invented traction).
2. **Thresholds are frozen.** Changing a threshold after data exists is recorded in `pivot_log.md` as a pivot decision, never edited in place.
3. Sequencing is forced: **E2 and E6 before E1** — now feasible on the slid calendar: E1 sessions start week 10, leaving weeks 1–9 for the ledger check, DSR computation, and the ≥50-seed harness (E2 and E6 are also *components* of the trust-test kernel, so they gate E1 twice over). **E1 before E4** (no paid ask before the trust test passes). E3 runs continuously from day 1, E7 parallel, E8 waits for ledger accumulation, E8-full waits for block C.
4. **Capacity slip rule (gtm §4 table, carried verbatim in spirit):** if the founder is over capacity, **paid-signal work (E4) slips first** — before community maintenance (the channel decays without it; the comp died of exactly this [C35]) and never at the expense of the trust test, which is the decision gate for everything downstream.
5. Every experiment feeds the same two instruments: the three loop numbers (bundles/mo × reproductions/bundle × reproduction→signup, gtm §2) and the north-star pair (kept improvements/week + reproduction rate, lean_canvas Key metrics).
