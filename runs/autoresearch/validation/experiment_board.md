## Board rules

1. **No row moves from "planned" without raw counts attached** — "went well" is not a result (quality-bar #1: mechanism over adjective; SKILL red flag: invented traction).
2. **Thresholds are frozen.** Changing a threshold after data exists is recorded in `pivot_log.md` as a pivot decision, never edited in place.
3. Sequencing is forced: **E2 and E6 before E1** (the bundles shown must be DSR-honest and the gates measured), **E1 before E4** (no paid ask before the trust test passes), E3 runs continuously from day 1, E7 parallel, E8 waits for ledger accumulation.
4. Every experiment feeds the same two instruments: the three loop numbers (bundles/mo × reproductions/bundle × reproduction→signup, gtm §2) and the north-star pair (kept improvements/week + reproduction rate, lean_canvas Key metrics).
