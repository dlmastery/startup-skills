# Stage gate — Ascent (Blank: Discovery → Validation → Creation → Building)

> **What this is** — Ascent's honest placement on the Blank stage ladder, with the five numeric criteria that open the next gate.
> **Why it exists** — a PoC validated across six domains invites the claim that the company is past Discovery. It is not, and a company selling rigor cannot overclaim its own stage. The file also relocates the 150-paying bar to the Validation exit, so a year of selling is not demanded inside a stage whose job is learning; the pre-order overlap is named, not hidden.
> **How to read it** — the evidence table's "What it does NOT prove" column is the point; a skeptic should attack the early-Validation overlap that runs $99 pre-orders during Discovery.
> **Depends on / feeds** — inherits from [experiment_board.md](experiment_board.md) and [riskiest_assumptions.md](riskiest_assumptions.md); feeds [metrics_by_stage.md](metrics_by_stage.md), [../financials/use_of_funds.md](../financials/use_of_funds.md).

## Current position: **Customer Discovery (with early-Validation overlap).** Working PoC, zero external validation — said plainly.

There are no external users, no revenue, no independent reproduction of any PoC result, and no completed customer interviews (A4, A6, survey §7). The company holds a working proof of concept validated across 6 domains *by the founder only*, a fully argued strategy layer, and a pre-assembled demand pool it has not yet touched. That is Discovery — nothing more — and claiming otherwise would violate the pack's own overclaim boundary (positioning §4).

**Why "with early-Validation overlap" (decision, critic round 2):** the $99 pre-order motion (E4, days 110–120) is selling — a Validation-stage activity by Blank's definitions — deliberately executed during late Discovery because it buys the same conversion evidence as a hosted build at ~5% of the cost (gtm §4 step 7); revenue_build stage 1 runs during late Discovery by design. The overlap is named rather than hidden so nobody mistakes 30 pre-orders for a validated, repeatable sales motion. Correspondingly, the **≥150-paying bar moves to the Validation → Creation gate** below: paying-customer *scale* is what Validation must produce before Creation, not what Discovery must produce before Validation — keeping it at this gate would have forced a year of selling inside a stage defined by learning.

## Exit criteria: Discovery → Validation (all numeric, all pre-declared; sources in experiment_board.md)

Gate opens only when **all five** hold:

1. **Trust test passed (E1, sessions weeks 10–13):** ≥5/15 practitioners reproduce a bundle unaided with one command AND ≥3/15 run their own private data AND ≥8/15 name specific deploy-evidence (gtm §4 / BRIEF thresholds, unchanged).
2. **Proof artifact honest (E2 + E6):** DSR ≥ 0.95 computed with true trial count, return skew/kurtosis, and track length T + CPCV published for the lead bundle (or the fresh-instrumented-run substitute, positioning §4.2), and gate catch-rates measured on ≥50 seeded violations per gate class (≥90% deterministic-class catch, ≤10% false positives).
3. **First money-based evidence (E4, window days 110–120): ≥30 $99 pre-orders AND ≥5 design partners** — the deliberate early-Validation overlap named above; this is conversion *signal*, not a repeatable motion.
4. **Kill triggers not fired at the corrected-active checkpoint (market_sizing §6, D-TELEMETRY denominator):** paid conversion ≥0.5% of corrected active users AND corrected active base >3,000 at month 6 (registry ÷ 0.35 opt-in midpoint, band 20–50%, methodology frozen in E3). Firing either does not advance the stage — it escalates A2 to the ASSUMPTIONS.md protocol.
5. **Discovery interviews done:** ≥20 synthesized interviews across cards 1–4 per discovery_guide.md, with must-have-language tallies confirming (or correcting) the value-prop #1 mapping.

## Later gates (numbers set now only where the pack commits them)

- **Validation → Creation:** **≥150 paying individuals ≈ $225k ARR run-rate** (market_sizing §5 — the corrected year-1 beachhead number, ~1.6% of the ~9.5k mid-band active base, inside the [C32] 1–5% norm; moved here from the Discovery exit per the overlap decision above — this is the scale Validation must produce); repeatable sales motion proven — the three loop numbers (bundles/mo × reproductions/bundle × reproduction→signup) stable or rising for 2 consecutive quarters; Team pull-through evidence (first 2–4% of payers converting teams, market_sizing §3); north-star pair moving together (kept improvements/week AND reproduction rate — either alone is vanity, lean_canvas Key metrics). Enterprise prerequisites funded: legal entity, security documentation, source escrow (market_type §4).
- **Creation → Building:** SOM-plan trajectory ($1.0M ARR / 36mo band $0.4–2.4M, market_sizing §6) holding without founder-led sales on every deal; first audit-trail-led quant/enterprise deals closing at $100k+ [C19] via bottom-up pull only.

## Verdict

**Discovery (with early-Validation overlap), early.** The single next gate that matters is the trust test — everything downstream (pre-orders, hosted build, Team tier, the entire revenue model) is explicitly sequenced behind it by the pack's own strategy layer. The honest one-line status for any investor conversation: *working PoC, pre-revenue, riskiest assumption scheduled for test in weeks 10–13 with pre-declared pass/fail numbers.*
