# Journey — A day in the life: Marcus, ordinary Tuesday, all touchpoints

> **What this is** — One ordinary Tuesday, 06:55 to 22:10, tracing every touchpoint and tallying the human minutes: about 24.
> **Why it exists** — Retention rests on how thin the human layer is — if a habitual day costs more than a few minutes, the technical-lead model and the $/day economics both fail. The day also defines activation health and shows the payer≠user export beat without claiming the year-2 attestation pack.
> **How to read it** — The 16:00 buyer beat and the 18:30 value review. Attack the 24-minute tally and whether 61 gate-passed experiments/week is demonstrated or projected.
> **Depends on / feeds** — Continues [beachhead.md](beachhead.md); walks [ux_spec.md](../ux_spec.md) screens in order; instruments [PRD.md](../PRD.md) §9 and [unit_economics](../../financials/unit_economics.md).

**Calendar anchor:** set post-launch with Next-tier features live (acceptance gate #25, Goodhart monitor #28, catalog #30) — consistent with beachhead.md's Week 12+ section.

**Persona:** Marcus T., solo quant (personas card 2), months into habitual use, two campaigns live (FX intraday; a new rates dataset). Payer = user, so the "buyer's view" here is Marcus reviewing his own spend and record-of-value — plus one beat where the artifact travels to someone who *is* a payer≠user buyer. The campaign has been running all night; the day shows how thin the human layer is by design (the technical-lead model, PoC §3.1; RE-Bench division of labor [B4]).

Format: *time — component → what happens → durable record.*

**06:55 — phone, in the kitchen** (paper App. B.3's mobile check, productized). **Local dashboard, phone-friendly page** — the `ascent dashboard` app served on his home network (ux_spec screen 12; hosted mirroring is a separate Next-tier option he hasn't enabled) → overnight summary: campaign A ran 34 trials, 2 kept; campaign B ran 29, 0 kept; spend $6.90 across both (mid-tier 91%) — consistent with his ~$7/day norm (beachhead beat 5). **Stall detector** flags campaign B: "last 12 experiments diminishing returns — suggested steering: research alternative label definitions." → *Record:* none; the phone is read-only by design — steering from a kitchen phone at 6:55 is how bias creeps in; he waits for the desk.

**07:40 — desk, coffee.** **Campaign dashboard (local)** → per-fold heatmap on campaign A: both kept trials improved the weak 2023 fold without degrading others; **Goodhart divergence monitor** green on A, mildly amber on B [D31]. **Trial ledger** → he reads the two kept blobs end-to-end (4 minutes; they're cited and self-contained) and skims three interesting discards — one rejected by the **trial-count-corrected acceptance gate** at N=241 with the deflated threshold printed on the card [P2]. → *Record:* none yet — reading.

**07:52 — steering, 2 sentences total.** **Steering console** → campaign B: "Self-reflect on the stall; the label may be the problem — ground a search on alternative horizon definitions before more feature work." Campaign A: nothing (healthy campaigns get silence). Constitution §49–52 binds the next Hypothesize on B to the directive. → *Record:* steering log entry, timestamped, linked to all descendant trials.

**08:00–12:30 — Marcus does his actual job.** Client call, reconciliations, reading. The **runner** grinds both campaigns through the 7-step loop; **CheckpointManager** writes state every 5 minutes; **Gate 1/2/3** verdicts accumulate on every trial. His laptop lid stays open in the corner; a thermal hiccup at 10:40 kills the process — **crash recovery** resumes from the 10:38 checkpoint with zero loss (P8; PoC resilience case study, founder-reported). → *Record:* continuous ledger rows, blobs, checkpoints; the 10:40 crash and resume are themselves logged in the hardware log.

**12:30 — lunch, one glance at the phone.** Campaign B's first post-steering trial: Ground step returned four verified papers on label-horizon choice [Gate 2 ✓]; hypothesis card predicts +0.06 composite from a triple-barrier-style relabeling. He smiles — that's the paper he'd have half-remembered and never dug up. → *Record:* the trial's citation set, verified links.

**14:15 — a new champion on campaign A.** He glances at the dashboard between tasks and the champion card is waiting — no push notification exists; champions surface on the next dashboard open, which for a twice-daily-glance user is never far. **Multi-seed confirmation** ran automatically: 5/5 seeds improve, spread ±0.005 [P6]. **Winner archiver** → bundle built: 14-section audit report, CPCV path distribution, reasoning blob, reproduction log; the honesty pair on the cover: "Sharpe 2.04 raw · 1.44 deflated over 243 trials." → *Record:* `winners/` bundle; org of one, but the shop's asset base just grew.

**16:00 — the buyer's view (payer ≠ user beat).** Marcus's outside capital comes from a small family office; its diligence officer — a David-type buyer (personas card 5) — has a standing quarterly ask. Old world: a PDF of curated backtests. New world: Marcus exports his **champion bundles** (the specced Pro surface — ux_spec screen 7; winner archive #5) with their built-in trial-ledger summaries (trial counts, discard totals, gate-verdict stats — the summary gtm §2 specifies for every bundle) plus reproduction instructions. Nothing here is the year-2+ Enterprise attestation pack (A10, features_prioritized #48 — roadmap, never claimed). The officer's analyst runs `ascent reproduce` on their own machine Thursday: pass under the declared tolerance policy, fingerprints equal. What the buyer sees is not a claim but a record — and what he *doesn't* see matters too: bundles carry champions and run logs, never Marcus's full negative-result ledger (A11 bundle rule — the discards summary is counts, not contents). → *Record:* export manifest noting exactly which artifacts left the machine, when.

**18:30 — spend and value review (Marcus as his own CFO).** **Spend meter** → month-to-date: ~$200 tokens at his ~$7/day norm (his own keys, A12; consistent with beachhead beats 5 and 9) + $129 Pro subscription. Against it, the ledger's month view: 61 gate-passed experiments/week vs. his manual 1–3/day ceiling [personas], two deployed champions, zero leakage incidents, and every number trial-count-labeled. The BRIEF's outcome metric — champion delta per dollar-day at zero leakage — is literally a dashboard tile. → *Record:* none; the review reads existing records. That's the point: nothing about proving value required extra work.

**22:10 — last look, lights out.** Phone: campaign B's relabeling line shows three trials, one kept — the stall broke. **Steering console** stays closed; the loop needs nothing from him until morning. Tomorrow's Diagnose will read tonight's Checkpoint, because the loop closes: *Sense state → Diagnose → Ground → change one thing → Measure → Keep-or-discard → Remember* (BRIEF core loop).

**Tally of human minutes: ~24.** Two sentences of steering, four minutes of blob reading, one export. Everything else was the constitution's discipline running on $6–7/day of mostly mid-tier tokens [B18][B22] — under 1% of what one day of his own time costs [C25-derived, value_prop_canvas §4.2].
