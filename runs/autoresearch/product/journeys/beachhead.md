## Week 0 — the skeptical trial (kernel surfaces only; this is the E1 script)

1. **Published champion bundle (gtm §2 Reproducible Champion loop)** → Marcus lands on the EUR/USD bundle from a forum post. The headline is not +6.52; it is "+6.52 raw · deflated X over 265 trials — founder-reported, full ledger attached" — the deflated figure computed by the **offline DSR script** (kernel #7), no dashboard needed (positioning.md §4.2). The self-deflation is what stops his scroll — every vendor shows him inflated backtests; nobody shows him the trial count. → *Record (his side):* none yet.
2. **`ascent reproduce`** (kernel #3) → one command on his own machine. Forty minutes later: **pass under the declared tolerance policy** — metrics inside the bundle's pre-declared epsilon bands, config/data fingerprints equal (not bitwise output equality; GPU/BLAS nondeterminism is stated up front). He inspects the ledger export (kernel #1): **runner-executed trials logged, kept and discarded, each with gate verdicts — with the honest caveat printed in the bundle that the ledger-completeness audit and the refusal ledger are week-1 build work (D-REFUSAL-LEDGER)**. He correctly discounts the raw Sharpe per [D6][D7] — and notes the system discounted it first. → *Record:* reproduction result; he accepts the **one-line registry ping consent** (kernel #9) — pass/fail + bundle id, nothing else — trust datum #1 for the network.
3. **Config hand-edit from a domain template** → his own intraday FX dataset, local only; he copies the EUR/USD fork's config and edits paths/metric by hand (he's exactly the user who can). **Minimal BYOK path** (kernel #8): his own API key via keychain/env on his OS; static economy routing. → *Record:* campaign config, dataset hash, routing config.
4. **Split verification (CLI)** (kernel #6) → walk-forward super-folds with 10-day purge/embargo on his label horizon [D8]; `validate_no_overlap()` prints the verification stamp into the campaign manifest. The check he'd have spent a week wiring by hand (persona pain 2) is a command. (The visual drag-to-adjust builder is launch tranche #13.) → *Record:* split config + verification stamp.

## Week 1 — first campaign

5. **Runner, overnight × 5 nights** → gate-passed experiments accumulate at ~$7/day on his own keys (P10; [B18][B22]). **Throughput, canonical numbers: the PoC sustained ~3–4 experiments/day (founder-reported, A6); 10–40 gate-passed experiments/night is a projection (assumption: shorter fixed-duration runs on his data + routing v1), to be measured, not promised.** → *Record:* ledger rows for every trial; reasoning blobs; 5-minute checkpoints.
6. **Offline DSR script, weekly** (kernel #7) → Friday he runs it against his own ledger: "champion Sharpe 1.87 raw · 1.31 deflated over 163 trials." He forwards the text output to his partner with one line: "finally a number I'd trade." → *Record:* DSR output stored beside the champion.
7. **Winner archiver** (kernel #5) → his first kept champion bundles automatically: 14-section audit report, embargo windows stated, trial count on the cover. → *Record:* `winners/` bundle.

## Weeks 2–6 — habit formation (launch-tranche surfaces)

8. **Morning ritual (7:40 am):** **campaign dashboard** (launch #10, the local `ascent dashboard` app) → ledger delta since midnight, champion status, **stall detector** (launch #17). Ninety seconds. **Steering console** (launch #18) → zero or one sentence. This is the whole human interface — the division of labor RE-Bench says is correct: the agent grinds short experiments; Marcus keeps the research judgment [B4]. → *Record:* steering log entries, linked to descendant experiments.
9. **Spend meter** (launch #20) → month view: ~$200 tokens on his own keys + the product fee once Pro exists (A12/A8 hypothesis). His "research log" is no longer a folder of notebooks (persona workaround); it is bundles plus a ledger he could show an allocator. → *Record:* none new; the review reads existing records.

## Week 12+ — Next-tier beats (labeled: these features do not exist at trust-test time)

10. **Trial-count-corrected acceptance gate** (Next #25) → **the conversion moment, kept, timeline-honest:** a candidate with +0.09 composite improvement is **discarded** — below the deflated threshold at N=163 (P2). Marcus's first reaction is annoyance; his second is the renewal decision. This gate is the thing he cannot make himself do at 11 pm on variant 40 (persona pain 1): the machine applies his own standards when he's tired. Until it ships, the offline DSR script covers the deflation story after the fact; the gate makes it *preventive*. → *Record:* rejection card in the ledger: "keep threshold at N=163 is +0.14; observed +0.09; discarded."
11. **DSR panel (UI)** (launch #16→Next polish) + **Goodhart divergence monitor** (Next #28) → amber banner: composite up, raw test Sharpe flat over the last 20 trials [D31]. Steering: "self-reflect on the stall; check if the drift penalty is dominating." → *Record:* divergence alert + steering entry.
12. **SOTA catalog subscription** (Next #30, his Pro renewal anchor) → monthly drop: two new time-series backbones with verified citations; he queues one. Fee logic per value_prop_canvas: rigor layer + catalog; tokens stay his own visible bill. → *Record:* catalog version pin in campaign manifest.
13. **Multi-seed + CPCV confirmation** (Next #26–27) → a champion candidate survives 5 seeds and shows a positive CPCV path distribution [D8][D11] (P6). He sizes a small live allocation — the outcome metric that matters (deployment action, PRD §9.3). Later, in a losing week, he confirms the drawdown sits inside the CPCV envelope he accepted at promotion time and types one steering line instead of panic-reverting: "research regime-detection literature for the next campaign." → *Record:* confirmation distributions in the bundle; steering entry seeding campaign 3.

**Why he stays (and pays):** not the hill-climbing — "my people can climb hills" is Elena's line, but Marcus's version is "I can climb hills; I can't referee myself at midnight." The retention spine is ledger (#1–2) + deflation (#7 now, #25 when it ships): his own discipline, externalized and tireless [A-series Tier 7].
