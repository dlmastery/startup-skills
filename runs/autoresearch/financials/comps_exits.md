## 2. Acquirer profiles (who pays, and why each would)

1. **Anthropic / OpenAI** — research-tooling absorption precedent set (Neptune→OpenAI [A33]); an agentic-research OS with a calibration corpus is a direct input to their own agent-training and eval loops. Pay for: the corpus, the constitution's encoded judgment, the team. Risk to price: they absorb capabilities cheaply when the target lacks distribution — Ascent's community + paying base is the price defense.
2. **CoreWeave (W&B)** — bought record-keeping for $1.7B [C34] and now owns a GPU cloud whose attach story is "the AI developer platform"; Ascent fills the layer W&B never had (experiments that design themselves) and drives GPU hours (hosted runs). Per-org math from their own deal: 1,400 orgs ≈ $1.2M/org; Ascent at even 300–500 orgs with a designing+auditing layer argues into the mid-hundreds of $M.
3. **Databricks / Snowflake** — data-platform attach: the customer's dataset already lives there; "point the research OS at the table you already govern" is a native cross-sell, and both buy at premium multiples for workload-generating layers (MLflow's steward Databricks pivoted it to GenAI observability [A32] — the experiment-design layer is conspicuously missing from their stack).
4. **Quant-infra buyers** (exchange/data groups, e.g. Bloomberg-adjacent, or a large systematic fund internalizing) — the vertical where audit gates are compliance features and seat prices run $32k/yr [C24]; a fund paying $100–250k/yr per alternative dataset [C37] can rationalize acquiring the audited-research capability outright. Smaller checks, strategic urgency.

## 3. IPO-path conditions (honest)

An IPO requires the **$100M+ conditional story** (revenue_build stage 4) to be actual, which requires every gated milestone before it: measured-active base ~10x (stage 3), Team attach ≥5%, enterprise DD package with reference customers, and — the load-bearing one — the corpus effect measured and compounding (A11), because public-market defensibility questions get asked with the open-source repo on the screen. Realistic shape: $100M+ ARR, 30%+ growth, 85%+ gross margin (unit_economics holds at scale by construction — BYOK), net retention >110% driven by Team/Enterprise expansion. That is a 7–10 year path with two conditional bends in it; the base plan of record ($1.0M at 36 months) does not promise it, and any deck slide claiming otherwise should be struck. The modal good outcome is the 15–50x strategic acquisition corridor in §1–2; the IPO is the tail that justifies venture pricing, stated as a tail.

## 4. Why this can be a standalone generational company

After the sober comps, the affirmative case. Every acquirer in §2 wants Ascent because it would own the layer their stack is missing — which is another way of saying the layer is a **control point**: nothing ships from an autonomous research loop without passing the gates, and whoever owns the gates owns the record of what works. The trackers died as features because they recorded without enforcing; AutoML collapsed because it searched without science; the paper factories legitimized the category without grinding anyone's real metric. Ascent sits at the one intersection with compounding economics on its side: a corpus that grows with every campaign (including the discards no fork can backfill), a reproduction network whose every node is both proof and distribution, ~90% gross margins that structurally *improve* as capability-per-dollar falls 40–50x/yr [B16], and a wedge customer (the solo quant) who upgrades into the fund, the lab, and eventually the university — the full spectrum on one system. If "every empirical field runs on research operating systems" is even half right (BRIEF vision), the git-of-experimental-research position is not a feature any acquirer can bolt on after the fact; it is a standard, and standards with private data exhaust behind them become generational companies — conditional on E8: the corpus effect must measure real (corpus-primed campaigns beating cold starts by the pre-declared ≥20%), or the "data exhaust" in that sentence is decoration and the compounding claim falls back to infrastructure and brand. The comps above price what Ascent looks like if it stalls at a layer; none of them price what it is if the loop, the ledger, and the trust brand compound together — that residual is the reason to build it independent, and to sell only if someone pays for the compounding, not the snapshot.
