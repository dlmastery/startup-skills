## Reading order for a diligencing investor

Risks 1–3 are the honest HIGHs and they are *different kinds*: #1 is the bet (priced into the stage-gating), #2 is the clock (priced into the raise size and speed), #3 is the structure (priced into the hire schedule). The pack's claim is not that these are low — it is that $1.2M buys a definitive answer to #1, meaningful distance on #2, and the first two hires against #3 (use_of_funds §2, §4).
