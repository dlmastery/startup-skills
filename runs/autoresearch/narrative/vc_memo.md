# Ascent — technical investor memo

All claims trace to the research layer (tags [A/B/C/D-n] resolve in `research/sources.md`) or carry an explicit assumption flag. PoC numbers are founder-reported, reproducible from the public repo, not independently verified (assumption A6).

## Thesis

Everyone building autonomous research systems is optimizing the wrong variable. Execution is solved — agents already medal on 40–65% of Kaggle-style ML tasks depending on scaffold and budget [A10][B13][D39] and hold ~12-hour autonomous coding horizons that double every four months [B2] — yet every system in the field asks the user to trust unverified output, and 46% of developers already refuse [C3]. The unfilled layer is judgment under rigor: leakage-proof evaluation protocols, trial-count honesty, tamper-evident metrics, programmatic pre-commit gates. Ascent ships that layer as the product — a binding constitution that turns a commodity coding agent into a research operating system whose every kept result carries the evidence it didn't cheat. The 94,800-star, maintainerless Karpathy autoresearch repo proves the demand [A1]; the zero of sixteen notable forks that added rigor prove nobody else is building the supply [A2].

## Why existing approaches are insufficient

**1. The Karpathy loop and its dormant 94.8k-star ecosystem.** autoresearch (March 2026) gave an agent a single-GPU LLM-training script and a ratchet rule — run fixed 5-minute experiments, commit only improvements — and collected 94.8k stars and 13.4k forks in six months, one of the fastest repo trajectories ever recorded [A1][A3]. It proved massive demand for the minimal loop and nothing else: one hard-coded task, one metric, no leakage protection, no literature grounding, no audit trail — none claimed. It has had no active maintainer since March 26, 2026, with ~185 issues and PRs accumulating unanswered [C35]. The community's own curated fork list is the decisive evidence: of 16 notable forks, 14 are hardware ports or translations, roughly one changes the research domain, and **zero add evaluation rigor** [A2]. Demand proven; rigor absent; supply abandoned (assumption A1-brief: repo stats re-verified 2026-08-27).

**2. The AI-scientist paper factories.** Sakana's AI Scientist legitimized the category to Nature-level credibility — first AI-generated paper past workshop peer review, methodology paper in Nature March 2026, company at $2.65B post-money [A13][A14][A15]. But independent evaluation (Beel et al.) found **42% of its experiments failed on coding errors**, its novelty checks misclassified textbook concepts as new, and its claimed autonomy leaned on human templates [A16]. Structurally, the whole family (Sakana, Agent Laboratory, Zochi, Carl) optimizes paper novelty and terminates in an artifact — it never grinds a user's fixed real-world metric, and reproducibility is secondary by design [A12–A22]. Sakana's own commercial positioning is Japan-optimized models [A15]; the AI Scientist is a flagship research program, not a product aimed at Ascent's buyer.

**3. ML-engineering agents.** AIDE/Weco, MLE-STAR, and the MLE-bench leaderboard race demonstrate real one-shot competence: published full-benchmark medal rates now span roughly **40–65%** depending on scaffold and budget (Operand Quant 39.6% [A10]; ML-ACE ~56% [B13]; MLEvolve 65.3% at a 12-hour budget [D39]). Two structural limits. First, every benchmark entry optimizes against a leak-free evaluator *built by the benchmark authors* — in the wild, constructing that evaluator is the hard part, and it is assumed away [A8]. Second, a 40–65% medal range means unsupervised correctness is far from solved; the remainder is exactly the failure mass audit gates exist to catch. MLE-STAR's one transferable finding cuts in Ascent's favor: retrieval-grounded agents measurably beat pure-LLM-prior agents on the same benchmark [A9].

**4. AutoML collapsed; tracking was absorbed.** The category leader's own trajectory is the post-mortem: DataRobot went from a $6.3B valuation to ~$500M with two layoff rounds — a 92% haircut on the thesis "automated search without scientific trust sells to enterprises" [A27]. H2O stalled and pivoted to sovereign GenAI [A24][A25]; Google shut down two of its own Vertex AutoML lines as Gemini absorbed them [A29]. On the tracking side, W&B was acquired into a GPU cloud at ~$1.7B and Neptune was acquired by OpenAI and shut down [A30][A33] — competent tools with happy users that could not sustain independent companies, because a tracker records what a human chose to run and enforces nothing: a leaky split logs as cleanly as a sound one. The pattern across both categories: **point solutions and features get absorbed; the defensible position is owning the whole loop** — which none of them ever did.

**5. The real enemy: manual + ChatGPT.** The dominant workaround today is a practitioner asking a chatbot for ideas and code, running experiments by hand, and judging results by eye [competitors row 19]. It costs $20–200/mo plus a salary, produces 1–3 experiments a day, and polices leakage, cherry-picking, and Goodharting with nothing but discipline — which fails at published scale (294 leaked papers across 17 fields [D5]). Nothing runs while the practitioner sleeps, chat suggestions arrive uncited, and the audit gate is a tired human. This is the behavior Ascent replaces, and it is universal. The one-line contrast from positioning: *your chatbot suggests; a tired human verifies — Ascent's protocol verifies; you steer.*

## Core architecture

1. **The constitution** — a binding 52-section protocol file (open source by design; the file is the channel, not the moat — A11) governing every agent action; rigor and vocabulary adapt per domain, with no "lite" fork.
2. **The 7-step loop** — Diagnose → Literature-ground → Hypothesize → Execute → Analyze → Document → Checkpoint, enforced on every experiment; one minimal change per fixed-duration run.
3. **Three audit gates** — programmatic pre-commit checks on data integrity, citation rigor, and reasoning completeness. Deterministic checks (citation exists, split geometry, hash integrity, ledger completeness) are the guarantee; LLM-judged checks are a screen, per the judge-reliability literature [survey §4.4].
4. **Composite metric, fingerprinted** — a task-tailored scalar, SHA-256-fingerprinted so keep-or-discard decisions are tamper-evident. Scope stated plainly: the ratchet is monotonic *in the proxy*; fingerprinting makes the proxy tamper-evident, not Goodhart-proof [D30][D31].
5. **Super-folds** — purged/embargoed evaluation splits (López de Prado lineage [D8]) making temporal/overlap leakage structurally impossible; semantic leakage classes are the gates' job, not the folds' [D5].
6. **Champion / winner archive** — only strict improvements dethrone the champion; every kept champion ships as a fingerprinted bundle (config, model, reasoning, logs) reproducible in one command.
7. **Steering layer** — high-level natural-language direction from the human technical lead; LLM-agnostic by design (A9), BYOK by pricing architecture (A12), checkpointed aggressively enough that a consumer laptop is viable research hardware [B26–B28].
8. **Roadmap gate, announced not shipped** — trial-count-corrected acceptance thresholds (a deflated-composite-metric analogue). No published system has one, Ascent's PoC included [survey §6]; first mover on that gate owns the standard the critics themselves defined.

## Two operating examples

**Marcus, solo quant, 11pm.** Fortieth signal variant this month; in-sample Sharpe looks great, and he knows that is the problem — with enough trials, a high in-sample Sharpe is near-guaranteed at a true Sharpe of zero [D7]. He points Ascent at his returns data and composite metric, sets the embargo window, and goes to bed. Overnight the loop runs a dozen audited experiments; two survive the gates and the purged folds; ten are discarded — *and the discards are the product*: the trial ledger records every attempt, so the deflated Sharpe is computable with the true trial count [D6]. In the morning Marcus doesn't read the champion number first. He reads the ledger — trial count, embargo geometry, discards included — because that ledger is the thing no notebook folder of his own has ever contained. The winner archive reruns with one command.

**Priya, computational-biology postdoc, private dataset.** Single-cell data, a clear AUROC target, no ML engineering depth, and a real fear: she once preprocessed on the full dataset before splitting — the exact leakage subtype in the Kapoor–Narayanan census [D5]. She runs Ascent on her laptop against her own private data — which no LLM's training set has seen, so the contamination objection that haunts public benchmarks [D37][D38] cannot touch the result. The constitution supplies the protocol she couldn't build: splits constructed before features, preprocessing inside folds, a cited rationale per experiment. What she takes to her PI is not a model but a validation record — every kept experiment with its run log and gate results — the anti-black-box.

## Why this can win where others haven't

**The empty quadrant.** Two axes divide this market: run terminus (one-shot artifact vs. sustained campaign on the user's own metric) and output trust (trust-me vs. audit-gated) [positioning §1]. Paper factories, AutoML, ML-engineering agents, coding agents, and manual+ChatGPT all sit in one-shot × trust-me; the Karpathy lineage is sustained × trust-me and hard-coded to one task; sustained × audit-gated is **empty** [positioning §2]. Incumbent trajectories point away from it: AutoML is being cannibalized, trackers were absorbed, coding agents chase general software work, AI-scientist companies chase publication milestones [A27–A36].

**The moat is not the file (A11).** The constitution ships open source — an open-core channel and a secret constitution are mutually exclusive. What accumulates and cannot be forked: (1) the **private cross-domain calibration corpus** — full trial ledgers *including discarded experiments*, gate catch-rates on seeded violations, what-worked-where priors; bundles publish champions and run logs, never the negative-result ledger, so the corpus never leaks through the channel; (2) **hosted trust infrastructure** — the DSR/attestation service, SOTA catalog, and the reproduction network of users who have rerun each other's bundles; (3) the **rigor brand**, compounded by public self-deflation. The window before a Cursor/Cognition/Anthropic-class team ships a "research mode" is honestly estimated at **12–24 months** [positioning §5] — and a fast follower starting at month 18 has 18 fewer months of ledger it cannot backfill, because you cannot reconstruct discarded experiments from campaigns you never ran.

**Self-deflation as strategy, not confession.** The pack's #1 scientific critique is the deflated-Sharpe/multiple-testing problem: a champion selected from 265+ trials is precisely what the DSR was invented to discount [D6][D7]. Ascent's answer is to publish the DSR-corrected number, with the true trial count and a CPCV path distribution, *before critics compute it* — "+6.52 raw, X deflated over N trials, here's the ledger." No competitor chasing benchmark headlines can copy this posture without deflating their own headlines; that asymmetry is the brand. (Computability caveat carried honestly: DSR needs the full discard ledger — if the PoC didn't log discards, the deliverable is a DSR on a fresh instrumented run, stated plainly [positioning §4.2].)

**Why now, compressed.** Agent horizons ~12h at 50% reliability, doubling every ~4 months [B2]; cost per unit of capability falling ~40–50×/yr, making 24/7 steering a $3–60/day line item [B16][B18]; 128GB-unified-memory laptops put the entire validated domain envelope on consumer hardware [B26–B28]; and the demand pool is pre-assembled and countable [A1].

## Honest risks

1. **Trust-to-payment conversion is unproven — the riskiest assumption.** 94.8k stars prove demand for a free toy, not for $1,500/yr of enforced rigor, against a backdrop of 46%-and-rising distrust of AI output [C3][market_type §3]. Mitigation: the 15-practitioner trust test (≥3 on private data) gates the paid build; $99 refundable pre-orders buy the conversion signal at ~5% of a hosted build's cost [gtm §4]; kill trigger pre-committed at <0.5% of measured active users [market_sizing §6].
2. **The composed system is unvalidated.** Each mechanism is individually literature-grounded (retrieval-grounding beats blind search [D28]; purge/embargo is settled methodology [D8]; the failure modes gates target are independently documented [A16][A46]) — but the composition is not: gate catch-rates on seeded violations are unmeasured, multi-seed variance unreported [survey §7]. Mitigation: instrument from day 1 — seed violations, measure catch-rates, log every discard; publish the numbers whatever they are (the self-deflation posture generalizes).
3. **Incumbent fast-follow.** Cognition ($26B), Anysphere ($29.3B), or a lab could ship a research mode within two model generations [A35][A36]. Mitigation: the 12–24-month window is spent building the three assets a follower cannot backfill (corpus, reproduction network, brand); watch item is explicit in the landscape [Tier 6].
4. **Key-person risk.** A pseudonymous solo founder (A5) is a single point of failure and fails bank/fund vendor DD — which is why enterprise/quant sales are gated to year 2+ behind a legal entity, security documentation, and source escrow [market_type §4]. Mitigation: the constitution externalizes the founder's judgment into an inspectable artifact — the product is precisely the founder's process made transferable — plus first hires per the team plan.

## Bottom line

The 2024–2026 literature converged on two findings: agents can now grind ML metrics better than humans over short horizons, and no one should trust what they produce without structural safeguards [D24][D26][D20][D30]. Ascent is the bet that the second finding is a product. The demand is countable, the mechanisms are individually evidenced, the quadrant is empty, and the honest number — not the flattering one — is the go-to-market. The next $1 of investor money buys the trust test that converts founder-reported into independently reproduced.
