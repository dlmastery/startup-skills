## ⚠ Riskiest cell: **Revenue streams** (with Customer segments as its twin)

The canvas hinges on: **practitioners will trust — and pay for — experiments they didn't run** (BRIEF riskiest assumption), against 46% and rising developer distrust of AI output [C3]. Everything else has external evidence (capability [B1–B4], free-demand [A1], channel economics [C31][C32], willingness-to-pay corridor [C23][C17]); the trust→payment conversion for autonomous research output specifically has none yet — stars prove a free toy's demand, not $1,500/yr rigor demand (market_type.md §3). See `validation/riskiest_assumptions.md` *(forward reference — file to be produced by the validation layer)*: the 15-practitioner test gates the paid build (gtm.md §4).

Second-order flag: the A11 moat assets are *accumulating* claims — the corpus moat exists only after instrumented usage at scale; until then defensibility rests on window speed (12–24 months, positioning.md §5.1) and brand.

## Decision

Canvas committed as above. Build order forced by the riskiest cell: (1) verify the discarded-trial ledger and run the trust test with DSR-honest bundles; (2) only on pass, open $99 pre-orders for BYOK Pro (no hosted build first); (3) Team/Enterprise features wait for bottom-up pull evidence. No enterprise sales motion before year 2.
