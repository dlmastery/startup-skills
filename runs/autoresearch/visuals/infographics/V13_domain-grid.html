Zone 4 — How to read these numbers
All founder-reported, single-seed in most domains, on public datasets with contamination caveats, reproducible from the repo — not independently verified. That framing is binding on every artifact in this pack. Third-party validation exists for the spec, not the results: an independent audit of open-source AI-scientist systems found four recurring failure modes, and this architecture addresses three, with the fourth named as roadmap.
