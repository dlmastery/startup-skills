What this journey is arguing
Retention does not come from the champion number. It comes from a morning that costs ninety seconds, a crash that costs nothing, and an export a skeptical third party can reproduce. Silence on a healthy campaign is a feature — it is what separates an operating system from a dashboard that needs attention.
