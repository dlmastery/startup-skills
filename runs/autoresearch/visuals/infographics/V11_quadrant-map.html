Zone 4 — Axes tested and rejected as primary
  • Price/cost locus (cloud vs laptop) — real, but divides cost structures, not buyer decisions. Retained as the secondary wedge.
  • Model/benchmark quality — the default axis in public comparisons and exactly where we must not fight: benchmarks carry contamination and conflate model, scaffold and budget.
  • Degree of autonomy — Devin and Cursor own it, and it is not what practitioners distrust. They distrust the output [C3].
