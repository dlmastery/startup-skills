BYOK pricing removes token costs from our margin by construction

Bring-your-own-key is the architecture, not a discount gimmick: tokens are user-side by definition, so Pro carries ~zero token COGS, the user keeps model choice as a platform-risk hedge, and their own bill stays small and legible.

Pro sits above Copilot/Cursor — which sell code completion, a commodity — and squarely inside the Claude-Max/Devin power band. Correct, because the job replaced is research iteration, not typing speed.
Zone 4 — Why a hosted flat rate would break
A hosted flat-rate Pro would carry $90–360/mo of token COGS against $125 of revenue — negative margin on exactly the best users, the ones who run the most experiments. BYOK inverts that: the heaviest user is the happiest customer, not the most expensive one.
Metering lands only where marginal cost is real — our GPUs, our tokens on hosted Team runs — and never on the rigor layer, which would tax the exact behaviour the product exists to enable.
Result: fully-loaded gross margin 82.0% at 150 users, ≥85% at 300+.
