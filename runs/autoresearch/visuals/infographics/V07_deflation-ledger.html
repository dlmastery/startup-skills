Our flagship number, deflated by our own audit — that's the brand

Every competitor leads with its best number. We lead with the discount applied to ours, and publish the ledger that makes the discount checkable. The honesty is not a disclaimer bolted on the end; it is the product's only durable differentiator.
Zone 1 — The only way this number ever renders
+6.52 raw (founder-reported) · deflated: week-1 work · N=265+ trials
never the raw number alone — binding on every artifact, slide and image in this pack
Zone 2 — Why the raw number cannot stand
  • A champion selected from 265+ trials is exactly the statistic the deflated Sharpe ratio exists to discount [D6][D7]
  • +6.52 is therefore an upper bound on the honest number, not a result
  • Enough trials guarantee a great in-sample Sharpe at a true edge of zero [D7]
Computability caveat, stated up front: the DSR requires the FULL trial ledger including discarded experiments. If the PoC did not log discards, the honest deliverable is "DSR computed on a fresh, fully instrumented run" — never a promise about the old number.
Zone 3 — The self-deflation protocol
1
Before any champion bundle ships publicly, compute the DSR at the true trial count plus a CPCV path distribution [D6][D8]
2
Make the corrected number the headline: "+6.52 raw, X deflated over N trials — here's the ledger."
3
If the deflated number is weak: lead with the fraud-vs-AutoGluon result and reposition EUR/USD as the methodology showcase. The plan survives a bad number; it would not survive a hidden one.
Zone 4 — The same discipline applied to every other claim
  • "Leakage structurally impossible" → scoped to temporal and overlap leakage only [D5][D8]
  • "Monotonic" → always monotonic in the composite proxy, never in the truth [D31]
  • Fingerprint → tamper-evident, not tamper-refusing (refusal is [ROADMAP])
  • All PoC metrics → founder-reported, single-seed, reproducible from the repo, not independently verified
Zone 5 — Why this is a moat and not modesty
The one unrecoverable error with a quant is overclaiming. A vendor who publishes the discount on their own headline number before anyone else computes it has bought the only currency that matters in this market — and a competitor cannot copy it without also giving up their best slide.
