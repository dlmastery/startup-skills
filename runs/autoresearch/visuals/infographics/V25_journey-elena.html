Journey — Elena runs parallel campaigns

Head of research at a systematic fund, year 2. The edge-high user is retained by the same property that converts the beachhead: the system tells the truth about itself.
MONTH 0
The trigger
In strategy committee, pod 3 answers "which variant produced this backtest?" by opening the champion bundle: config, cited reasoning, embargo windows, trial count 214, DSR beside the raw Sharpe.
The meeting that historically died in reconstruction ends in 20 minutes.
MONTH 1
The honest security review
Training and eval data never leave fund hardware — execution is local by architecture.
But steering currently calls hosted LLM APIs. BYO-endpoint and local-model steering are ROADMAP; attestations ROADMAP.
MONTHS 2–3
Stretching the power users
6 concurrent streams across two pods. Her validation group runs the seeded-violation harness themselves — their catch-rates match the vendor's published scorecard.
Family-level acceptance: a pod's champion must clear the deflated threshold at the family's cumulative N.
MONTH 4
The deal
Immutable 7-year retention configured. Her team opts OUT of corpus contribution and negotiates corpus access as a contract term.
She signs Team for two pods, with an option tied to the BYO-endpoint milestone.
“The vendor said the uncomfortable part unprompted.”
— her infrastructure lead, on the security review
Why disclosure closed the deal instead of losing it
Every vendor in this category claims local execution. Ascent named the one place data does leave — reasoning tokens to hosted APIs — before the security team found it.
That single disclosure converted an adversarial review into a roadmap negotiation, and it is why the contract has an option attached to a milestone rather than a signature withheld pending one.
The capability no evaluated competitor had
Family-level acceptance. When six streams run in parallel, each pod's champion is being selected from the family's cumulative trial count, not its own — so a pod-local threshold is systematically too generous.
No internal platform and no vendor she evaluated corrects for this.
Stated in scope: family-level thresholds depend on the aggregate trial count no tracker currently offers, and the acceptance gate itself is ROADMAP.
