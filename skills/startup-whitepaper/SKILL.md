# startup-whitepaper

## WHITEPAPER.md

How this makes the core job `{problem.10x_claim}` — mechanistically.

Required:

- Current inefficiency (`problem.industrial_trap`)
- Core mechanisms (`problem.10x_mechanism` unpacked)
- Full spectrum: `{spectrum.low_end}` through `{spectrum.high_end}`
- Every modality on `{spectrum.modality_axes}`
- Grounding and trust as multipliers
- Projected outcomes + evidence base
- Risks and mitigations

A 10x claim without a mechanism is marketing. Each 10x factor names a loop, a model, or a measurement.
