# Grill question bank

Curated founder-interrogation questions, by domain. Pick the highest-uncertainty ones; adapt nouns to the idea. Never ask all of these — this is a quiver, not a script.

## 1. Problem & pain
- Describe the exact moment the problem occurs. Who is standing where, doing what?
- How is this solved today, step by step? What does that cost in time/money/errors?
- Is this a vitamin, painkiller, or oxygen? What breaks if they simply don't solve it?
- How often does one user hit this? Daily pain beats annual pain.
- What have people already duct-taped together? (Workarounds are demand evidence.)

## 2. Users & spectrum
- Who is the *first* user — one named archetype, not a segment?
- Who is the low-support edge (least able, most constrained user)? The elite edge (most demanding power user)? Can one system serve both without separate tracks?
- User ≠ customer ≠ payer: who feels the pain, who evaluates, who signs?
- Who loses if you win? (They will fight you.)

## 3. Why now
- What changed in the last 18 months — model capability, cost curve, regulation, behavior — that makes this newly possible?
- Why did previous attempts at this fail? Name them.
- If it's such a good idea, why hasn't {biggest incumbent} done it? What's your answer beyond "they're slow"?

## 4. Wedge & vision
- What is the smallest slice you can do 10x (not 20%) better than the status quo?
- What does the company look like in 10 years if everything works? What's the headline?
- What do you deliberately NOT do in year one?

## 5. Moat & compounding
- What gets better with every user: data, matches, content, cost? Show the loop.
- After 2 years, what would a well-funded copycat still lack?
- What's the switching cost once a user is embedded?

## 6. Competition
- Name 5 alternatives, including "do nothing" and spreadsheets. Why does each fail — mechanism, not marketing?
- Where would you place competitors on the two axes that matter most in this space? What are those axes?
- What will {incumbent}'s counter-move be within 12 months of your traction?

## 7. Business
- Who pays, how much, how often? What's the comparable line item in their current budget?
- Is price anchored to cost saved, revenue created, or status conferred?
- Sales motion: self-serve, sales-led, partner-led? Who is the economic buyer and what do they need to see?

## 8. Founder edge
- What do you know about this problem that a smart generalist doesn't?
- What have you already built, sold, or lived through in this space?
- Why will you win the talent and distribution fights?

## 9. Riskiest assumption
- Write the one sentence that, if false, kills the company. How would you test it in 2 weeks for under $1k?
- What evidence would make YOU walk away?
